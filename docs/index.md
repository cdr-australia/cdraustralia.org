## 🔎 The Components of a Successful CDR

A complete and compliant CDR consists of several critical documents that must work seamlessly together:

1. **Three Career Episodes (CEs)**: Each episode focuses on a specific period of engineering activity or a distinct project.
2. **Summary Statement (SS)**: The most crucial page that links your career episodes to the required competency indicators.
3. **Continuous Professional Development (CPD)**: A one-page list formatted in chronological order detailing your post-graduation learning.
